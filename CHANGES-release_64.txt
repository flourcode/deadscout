DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 64
=======================
The ship notices her. Nothing to do, nothing to win — it just answers.

Presence
- Rooms warm as she enters. In the dimmer scenes the room she is in lifts
  by half and settles back about six seconds after she leaves. (Quiet Drift
  is already fully lit, so there is nothing to see there.)
- She carries a faint amber glow of her own, indoors only.
- Inner doors slide open as she comes near and close a moment after.
- Crew sense something: pass close to one and a small "…" then "?" appears
  over their head. At most once every 45 seconds each.
- K9 knows it is her: a small heart and a chirp when she comes close, at
  most once every 30 seconds.
- A curl of steam rises off the meal unit when she drifts past it.

Fixed
- Touch notes and the reactions above could not fire in the first seconds
  after the page loaded.

Not in this release
- Crew do not change what they are doing; the mark is the whole reaction.
- No leaning plant (there is no plant aboard) and screens do not flicker
  awake; they still answer with the note and the ring.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v64).
