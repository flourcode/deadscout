DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 85
=======================
Added
- The jelly in the tank is a comic. It gets no respect. Tap the tank and
  it tells a one-liner: a caption at the top of the picture, the jelly
  gives a wiggle, then a soft rimshot (two notes and a brush, in the key
  of the tape). Now and then Scout laughs.
- If the astronaut stays by the tank for a few seconds, the jelly tries
  one unprompted (it has an audience), at most every minute or two.
- 22 original jokes, shuffled; none repeats until all have been told.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v85).

DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 84
=======================
Fixed
- Scout no longer snaps back to the shaft on the way to the astronaut in
  the opening. Two causes, both fixed:
  1. The engine mislaid a walker's position when a walk was redirected a
     second time: it put them back where the first redirect happened.
     Any walk redirected twice could do this, not just the opening.
  2. The astronaut was calling Scout over again every few seconds while
     Scout was already on the way. Now it waits until Scout has stopped.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v84).

DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 83
=======================
Changed
- The welcome screen on upright phones and tablets. The scene now fills
  the whole screen: the same tall slice of the ship you get once aboard, a
  little closer. The title lies over the top of it and COME ABOARD over
  the bottom, in thumb reach. It used to be a small wide picture with the
  title above, empty dark below, and the button at the very bottom.
- Desktop and sideways phones are unchanged.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v83).
