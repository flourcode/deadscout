DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 82
=======================
Upright phones and tablets, and three smaller things.

Upright
- Aboard on an upright phone or tablet, the picture now fills the whole
  screen: a tall slice of the ship, as tall as the hull, about one and a
  half rooms wide. (It was a small wide picture at the top with empty sky
  below.)
- The camera follows the astronaut, so tapping to explore also moves the
  view.
- The bar lies over the bottom of the picture and fades when idle; a tap
  brings it back.
- Pinch in for the whole ship (small, with sky above and below); pinch
  out for the close view.
- The opening captions are laid out for the tall picture, on a dark band.
- Turn the device sideways and it goes back to the whole-ship picture.
- One picture pixel is two screen points, the same chunk as the
  whole-ship view on a desktop.

Also
- Link preview: the picture shown when the link is shared is now the
  orange astronaut and Scout on the welcome screen (og.png).
- Less motion: if the device asks for reduced motion, there is no roll
  outside, no gas puffs, no shooting stars or meteor shower, the dust
  hangs still, and the interface does not fade. (?calm=1 to try it.)
- Scout's colours: checked, nothing changed. Scout is already cream, with
  orange joints and a teal eye.
- A tap now brings the bar back on touch screens (it needed a mouse move).

For the maintainer
- run/suite.sh is the release check now. The old smoke.js tested menus
  that were removed in release 63.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v82).
