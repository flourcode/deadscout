DRIFTNAUT RADIO — RELEASE 91
============================
Renamed
- Adrift FM is now Driftnaut Radio. The opening screen's wordmark is
  DRIFTNAUT, over the same line: A LO-FI RADIO, A LONG WAY OUT.
- "Driftnaut Radio" is used wherever the name is written out: browser
  title (Driftnaut Radio — Lo-fi, a long way out), social tags, app
  manifest (home-screen name: Driftnaut), lock-screen track info, About,
  Privacy & Terms, the Director.
- Address is https://driftnaut.com/ again; the YouTube link is
  youtube.com/@driftnaut.
- Preview image re-made with the DRIFTNAUT wordmark.
- Nothing else changed from release 90. Cache bumped (driftnaut-v91).

ADRIFT FM — RELEASE 90
======================
Changed
- Ship sounds and tap sounds are on by default again, along with the
  music. Either can be switched off in the speaker panel for music
  alone; a choice already made there is kept.
- Scout stays quiet (no chirps). With tap sounds on, petting Scout gives
  the soft mechanical tick.
- Director: SHIP SOUNDS is ticked by default; untick it to record music
  alone. A broadcast link no longer alters the saved sound settings of
  the browser it is opened in.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v90).

ADRIFT FM — RELEASE 89
======================
A radio first
- Music plays alone by default. Ship sounds (ambience, machinery, room
  tone) and tap sounds are both off until turned on in the speaker panel.
- Scout is quiet. The chirps are gone: Scout makes no sound unprompted.
  Petting Scout gives a wag and a heart; with Tap sounds on, also a soft
  two-part mechanical tick.
- Now Playing, Skip and the speaker stay on screen at low contrast when
  the rest of the bar fades, so a track can be skipped at a glance. The
  track name is larger (14 px).
- The "Click a room to explore" prompt is removed. Hover marks remain on
  desktop; on touch, a first visit gets two brief corner marks and
  nothing else.
- A little more happens unattended: a surprise every 95 to 165 s (it was
  140 to 240).

The pitch
- Scout is aboard but out of the ad: the search and social description
  is now "A live lo-fi station aboard a little pixel spaceship. Leave it
  on while you work, read, or wind down, and watch life aboard the
  Halcyon." About no longer says "Pet Scout".

Director
- Broadcast links record music alone unless SHIP SOUNDS is ticked.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v89).

ADRIFT FM — RELEASE 88  (formerly Driftnaut; built on release 87)
================================================================
Renamed
- Driftnaut is now Adrift FM everywhere a visitor can see: the opening
  screen (ADRIFT FM / A LO-FI RADIO, A LONG WAY OUT / COME ABOARD, with
  "Listen to lo-fi. Watch life aboard the Halcyon." beneath), browser
  title, search and social description, canonical URL
  (https://adriftfm.com/), social tags and image, app manifest, the
  lock-screen and mini-window track info (it still said NIGHT WATCH),
  About, the Privacy & Terms page, and the Director.
- About uses the new copy, with a quiet YouTube · @adriftfm link and the
  Privacy & Terms link. The privacy page is rewritten for what the site
  does now, and says the YouTube link leaves the site.
- The old title menu behind the opening screen no longer offers GAME or
  THE STORY.
- Kept as they were so nothing saved is lost: preference keys, the
  first-visit key and the offline cache name prefix.

The first ten seconds
- The opening is shorter: the hull opens after 2.8 s (it was 7), and the
  two captions end as it finishes. The ship can be touched about 7 s
  after COME ABOARD (it was about 14).
- The music comes up as soon as the ship is on screen, not 2.6 s later.
- Switches work as soon as their room's lamps have come up; there is no
  fixed lockout any more.
- "Tap a room to explore" (touch) / "Click a room to explore" (mouse)
  appears as soon as the ship can be touched, on a first visit only, and
  goes at the first touch.
- Scout now starts on its cushion in the cabin, beside the astronaut.

Sound
- The three-state SOUND button is replaced by a speaker that opens a
  small panel: volume, mute, ship sounds, tap sounds. Turn the last two
  off for music alone. All of it is remembered; unmuting restores the
  last volume. M mutes.
- One NOW PLAYING label, in the bar. It and the speaker stay on screen,
  dimmer, when the rest of the bar fades.
- "Ship only" (music off) is gone.

One tap, one action
- A tap is for one thing: Scout, a bot, a loose object or the tank
  answers; a lamp or machine switches; anything else sends the
  astronaut. An object tap no longer also moves the astronaut.
- Scout, the tank and the cabin lamp have real buttons over them: a
  generous target, corner marks on hover and keyboard focus, a name for
  screen readers, and a brief first-visit cue on touch. Scout wags, the
  tank's glass ripples, the lamp switches.
- A longer walk shows a small marker where it ends.
- Dragging with a mouse still leads the astronaut; letting go over a
  lamp does not switch it.

Hands off
- A first-minute routine: the lamps come up, Scout trots over, the
  astronaut goes up to the bridge window and then makes a cocoa (on an
  upright phone the astronaut stays with Scout). Any tap ends it.
- The first surprise comes inside the first minute (it was 108 s).
- The jelly only volunteers a joke when you sent the astronaut to the
  tank. Tapping the tank still gets one.

Phone
- Upright: a VIEW button switches between the room (closer than before,
  and steady: it only slides when the astronaut nears the room's edge)
  and the whole ship.

The bar
- NOW PLAYING · SKIP · speaker · SCENE · (VIEW on upright screens) · a
  "more" button holding MINI WINDOW, FULL SCREEN and ABOUT.

Also
- An existing install picks up the new build on its next visit and
  swaps its offline copy (cache driftnaut-v88).
- New files: game/tap.js, game/fm.js.

DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 86
=======================
Fixed
- The ship's switches work again. When the reticle marks were removed in
  release 77, the switches went with them, because the marks were the
  buttons. Now the thing itself is the target and it answers at once,
  without waiting for the astronaut to get there:
    a ceiling lamp        that room's light on or off
    the shaft junction    every light aboard, bay by bay
    the reactor           drive: idle, full burn, shutdown, idle
    nav, sensor, comms    the screens come alive and the machine answers
    the two terminals     keys and a readout
    the service pumps     they cycle
    the airlock hatches   open with a hiss, then close
    the beacon mast       beacon on or off
    the bridge window     a look at the ship from outside
- No marks, labels or hints. A short caption names what happened where it
  is not obvious (DRIVE · SHUTDOWN, HAILING · OPEN CHANNEL).
- Scout, the bots, the loose objects and the jelly still answer for
  themselves. Nothing can be switched during the opening.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v86).

DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 85
=======================
Added
- The jelly in the tank is a comic. It gets no respect. Tap the tank and
  it tells a one-liner: a caption at the top of the picture, the jelly
  gives a wiggle, then a soft rimshot (two notes and a brush, in the key
  of the tape). Now and then Scout laughs.
- If the astronaut stays by the tank for a few seconds, the jelly tries
  one unprompted (it has an audience), at most every minute or two.
- 22 original jokes, shuffled; none repeats until all have been told.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v85).

DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 84
=======================
Fixed
- Scout no longer snaps back to the shaft on the way to the astronaut in
  the opening. Two causes, both fixed:
  1. The engine mislaid a walker's position when a walk was redirected a
     second time: it put them back where the first redirect happened.
     Any walk redirected twice could do this, not just the opening.
  2. The astronaut was calling Scout over again every few seconds while
     Scout was already on the way. Now it waits until Scout has stopped.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v84).
