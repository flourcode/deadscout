YOUR LITTLE PLACE IN SPACE

DRIFTNAUT is a cozy pixel spaceship that plays warm space-chill music. Come aboard and leave it running while you work, read or wind down. HALCYON is a research vessel, drifting peacefully a long way out. It is home to an astronaut and a robodog called Scout. The ship picks the music.

LISTEN
SCENE changes the ship's lights and mood (the music carries on) · SKIP plays the next track · SOUND steps through music + ship, ship sounds only, and off · your keyboard's and phone's media keys work too
EXPLORE
Click or tap anywhere to explore: the astronaut goes there by the ship's own ways, through the doors and up and down the shaft. Point outside the hull for a spacewalk through the airlock; point back in to come home. The arrow keys work too. Left alone, the astronaut keeps watch, and turns in at night
TOUCH
Every click is a note, higher up the ship is higher in pitch, always in the key of the track that is playing. Things answer with a little phrase of their own, and the ship works: lamps, the drive, the consoles, the pumps, the hatches. Scout keeps the astronaut company

GOOD TO KNOW

It runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded or tracked, and it works offline after one visit. The music is made live in the page, so it never repeats and is yours to use in a video or a stream. It remembers your scene and sound.
