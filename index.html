THE SHIP

DSV HALCYON, deep survey vessel, registry DSV-0417. Presumed lost with all hands in 2291, 167 AU from Sol. Decades later a repeating low-power transmission was detected near her last position. DeadScout is what answers when you link to it: a living ship to walk, to listen to, and to leave running.

ENTER HALCYON and you wake aboard, in the crew quarters, with a service robot at your heel. Read the terminals. Find the scanner and tune it into what the ship remembers. Play the crew's tapes. Notice what the cameras don't. There is no clock and nothing hunts you; the ship simply changes the longer you stay.

NIGHT WATCH is where the ship starts: press start and HALCYON runs herself, crew routines, screens, lamps, weather outside, her lights following your own clock. Leave it on. Click anything: the monitors, the plant, the mug on the comms shelf, the ladder (it plays), the stars, the crew. Hold the bridge glass. Double-tap a floor and K9 comes to sit. SLEEP settles the ship for the night and fades to black; FOCUS keeps a study timer on the bridge. SETUP chooses the view, mood, music and length; TAKE CONTROL hands you the commander.

TRANSMISSION NOTES

DeadScout runs entirely in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and after one visit it opens without a network. Your transmission saves itself as you go; CONTINUE picks it up where you left it. Finishing one unlocks a second.

The music is six tapes played by a band that lives in the page: no recordings, generated as you listen, the same hour from the same seed. The ship's ambience, the footsteps and the relays are made the same way. Everything you hear is yours to use in a video or a stream.

PHOTO MODE (Options) saves the frame exactly as the ship draws it, at up to 4K. RECORD A TAKE captures the picture and the sound to a video file, made on your computer.

If the picture tears, a screen says the wrong thing or the ship counts someone who isn't there, that is HALCYON. If it stops altogether, that is your browser: reload the page and carry on from your save. Long watches depend on the computer staying awake and the tab staying open.
