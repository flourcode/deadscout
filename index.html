YOUR LITTLE PLACE IN SPACE

DRIFTNAUT is a cozy pixel spaceship that plays warm space-chill music. Press LISTEN and leave it running while you work, read or wind down: the crew of HALCYON go about their day, the drones keep the ship tidy, K9 potters about. Press PLAY and drift through the ship yourself.

DRIFT
Move around the ship · arrow keys, or the on-screen pad
TOUCH
Click anything: screens, lamps, the plant, the mug, the ladder (it plays), the reactor · E uses what's nearby
LISTEN
SCENE changes the ship's mood and music · TAPE changes the music · SOUND balances music and noise · AUDIO ONLY turns the screen off

GOOD TO KNOW

It runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded or tracked, and it works offline after one visit. The music is made live in the page, so it never repeats and is yours to use in a video or a stream. It remembers your scene, tape and sound. There's a short story too (MENU · THE STORY), if you ever want one.
