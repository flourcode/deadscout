DRIFTNAUT — RELEASE 77
=======================
One layout aboard, from the UI review.

The picture fills the window (desktop and phones held sideways)
- No frame, no margin, no whole-number scaling steps. 1440 x 900 was
  1280 x 720 with a gap all round; it is now 1440 x 810. An ultrawide
  2200 x 900 window was 1280 x 720; it is now 1600 x 900.
- Where the window is not 16:9, the leftover strip is the same dark sky
  with a few stars (not the grid), and the picture's edge fades into it.
- The picture is drawn at the next whole multiple up and scaled down, so
  every picture pixel is the same size (very slightly softer than before
  at in-between sizes).

One bar
- SCENE, SKIP, SOUND, then three quieter ones: MINI (the floating window,
  was FLOAT, was parked under the clock), FULL (full screen, new) and ?
  (About, new).
- MINI and FULL are hidden where the browser cannot do them (phones).
- ? opens the About text as a panel over the ship; CLOSE, Esc or a click
  outside closes it. It used to sit below the picture, out of reach once
  aboard.

Phones and tablets
- Button text is 12 px (was 8). Every control is at least 44 px tall
  (48 on phones, 52 on tablets; was 36).
- Buttons keep their words: SCENE and SOUND sit as a small caption above
  the value.
- The FLOAT button no longer overlaps SKIP on tablets (it is gone there).

Removed
- The clock.
- The room markers (brackets on hover). Everything is clickable anyway.

Moved
- The hint sits just above the bar on desktop, just below it on upright
  phones and tablets.

Not in this release
- Upright phones and tablets still show the 16:9 picture at the top with
  empty sky below. Filling the height needs the renderer to draw a tall
  picture; see the note in the hand-off.
- Cache bumped (driftnaut-v77).
