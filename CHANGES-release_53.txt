  - warm Rhodes, gently strummed, with a few cents of tape wobble
  - round bass on the root, sitting under the keys
  - swung, laid-back boom-bap beat (soft kick, rimshot/snare/brush, quiet hats),
    lowpassed, gently ducking the keys
  - a two-bar pentatonic hook on a soft bell with a short echo, now and then
  - vinyl crackle, a small warm room, nothing above ~7 kHz
  - 66-82 BPM; two bars of keys before the beat comes in

  SPACE WAVE (78), SLOW CURRENT (74, two-chord float), QUIET ENGINES (70,
