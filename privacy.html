PRIVACY & TERMS

WHAT STAYS WHERE

Driftnaut Radio runs entirely in your browser. There is no account and no sign-in. Nothing you do aboard is sent anywhere.

What it remembers, it keeps in this browser's own storage, on this device: your scene, your volume and sound settings, and whether you have already seen the first hint. The files it needs are kept in the browser's cache, so after one full visit it opens without a network. A take you record from the Director's tools is written to your downloads and, while it is being made, to the browser's private file storage, which is cleared the next time you open the site.

We don't use cookies, analytics or trackers. The page, its fonts and its art are all served from this site.

The YouTube link in About opens YouTube, which is a separate site with its own privacy policy. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you follow that link.

To clear everything, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

THE SOUND AND THE PICTURE

The music and the ship's ambience are generated in your browser from oscillators, noise and filters, with no samples or recordings. Photos and takes you save are yours: use them in videos, streams, posts or anything else, personal or commercial, with no fee and no need to ask. Because anyone with the same seed can make the same hour of sound, it isn't exclusively yours: please don't register it with a fingerprinting service, claim exclusive ownership of it, or resell it as a sound library.

BROADCAST LINKS

A broadcast link made in the Director carries only its settings (format, scene, music, pace, seed). Anyone you give the link to can open those settings. It carries nothing about you.

NO WARRANTY

Driftnaut Radio is provided as it is. Long sessions and recordings depend on your browser and computer staying awake and the tab staying open.
