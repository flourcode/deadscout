PRIVACY & TERMS

WHAT STAYS WHERE

DeadScout runs entirely in your browser. There is no account and no server behind it. Your images, logos, tapes and settings are kept in this browser's own storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) and never leave your computer. Videos are made on your computer and saved straight to your downloads.

A tape's link carries its settings (sound, seed, look, words, format and length), never its image. Anyone you give the link to can open those settings.

We don't use cookies, analytics or trackers. The page and its fonts are served from this site; the YouTube link opens YouTube in a new tab, under YouTube's own policies.

To clear everything, delete your tapes in MY TAPES, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

THE SOUND

The ambience is generated in your browser from oscillators, noise and filters, with no samples or recordings, and it's royalty-free. Use them in your videos, streams, podcasts or anything else, personal or commercial, with no fee and no need to ask.

Because anyone with the same seed can make the same hour of sound, it isn't exclusively yours. Please don't register it with YouTube Content ID or any other fingerprinting service, claim exclusive ownership of it, or resell it as a sample pack or sound library.

YOUR IMAGES

Use images you have the right to use. DeadScout adds atmosphere to your image; it doesn't change who owns it.

NO WARRANTY

DeadScout is provided as it is. Long recordings depend on your browser and computer staying awake and the tab staying open.
