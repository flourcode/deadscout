                 the stage replays with its after-effects. (`a.pending`, `a.stage`, `a.locked`)

                 stay dark for ever after CONTINUE).

                 restore() ignores unknown ids.

                 tune-weak needs the scanner; signal-layer passes a duration to music('memory'); eng-memory-hint only
                 during the engineering memory; self-lock's jitter is rolled once per firing, not once per frame.
- cut.js         A locked door stops her (click-to-move and scripted moves walked straight through and unlocked it):
                 she walks to it, reaches, a locked use is recorded (the latch sound). Doors on one leg are handled in
                 walking order; the door's state is read at arrival time.

- cut.js         snapshot() stores the commanded room state, not the derived 'present-dark' (which, replayed by trim()
                 every 60 commands or by CONTINUE, silenced every later soft room state).
